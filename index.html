How to read the comparison

In the unfiltered 5,000-trader sample, both gains and losses extend farther from zero at higher volumes. More activity does not by itself identify a profitable trader.

43.74% of the unfiltered sample have positive P&L, compared with 43.70% in the full dataset. This agreement on one measure does not establish that rare traders or extremes are represented.

Filters apply to the sample. Color separation may reflect how the source assigned labels; it is not independent evidence of skill. The plot shows an association, not causation or return on invested capital.
